# Sealed: traction kit

The Superteam India judges score early traction. The goal before Oct 11: **5+ teams contacted with
evidence of interest, and 2+ devnet pilots or letters of intent.** These are templates; send them
yourself, and only record what people actually said.

## Who to contact

- Indian Web3 startups and DAOs that pay contributors in stablecoins (Superteam India members,
  hackathon winners, grant programs).
- Solana ecosystem teams paying global contributors in USDC.
- Remote-first companies already paying contractors in crypto.

Look for teams whose payroll or grants wallet already shows public USDC payouts: they have the
problem today.

## First message (X DM, Telegram, email)

> Hi [name], I'm building Sealed, private stablecoin payroll on Solana. When teams pay in USDC,
> every salary is public forever. Sealed uses Solana's Confidential Balances (re-enabled in June), so
> only the company, the employee and the accountant see amounts.
>
> You pay [contributors/your team] on-chain. Would you have 15 minutes this week to tell me how you
> handle it today? I'd love your honest take, and I can show a 2-minute devnet demo.

Follow-up after 3 days if there's no reply:

> Quick nudge on this. Even a one-line answer helps: does salary visibility on-chain matter to your
> team, or not really?

## 15-minute call script

1. **How do you pay people today?** Which token, which chain, how often, how many people?
2. **Who can see what?** Have team members, competitors or others noticed salaries or grants on-chain?
   Has it caused any problem?
3. **What have you tried?** Private wallets, off-chain payroll, mixing, just accepting it?
4. **Demo** (2 minutes): run payroll, the explorer view, the employee view, the accountant CSV.
5. **Reaction:** would you use this on mainnet? What would stop you? (Custody, compliance, USDC
   support, wallets.)
6. **Pilot:** would you run one mock payroll on devnet with your team, or sign a short letter of intent?
7. **Price:** what would a per-employee monthly fee need to be for this to be a no-brainer?

## Letter of intent (non-binding)

> **Letter of intent: Sealed private payroll**
>
> [Company], which pays [number] contributors in stablecoins, is interested in using Sealed for
> payroll when it launches on Solana mainnet, subject to a security audit and to [conditions, e.g.
> USDC support, Squads approvals].
>
> Our reason: [in their words, e.g. "we don't want every contributor's pay to be public"].
>
> This letter is not binding and creates no obligation for either party.
>
> [Name], [Role], [Company], [Date]
